About MCC School

A Legacy of Enlightened Excellence

Founded in 1837 by the American Mission, MCC Higher Secondary School stands as one of Chennai's most revered educational institutions. For nearly two centuries, we have nurtured generations of scholars, leaders, and visionaries who have shaped the fabric of modern India.

Our storied campus in Chetpet blends colonial heritage with contemporary rigour — a rare institution where tradition catalyses transformation, and every student discovers their fullest potential beneath vaulted ceilings and blue Tamil Nadu skies.
